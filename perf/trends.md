# TikTok trend recipes for CertTrack

Refreshed weekly by the "CertTrack trend refresh" scheduled task (last refresh: 2026-11-09).
Each recipe takes a format that is getting big views on TikTok right now and gives it a CertTrack twist.
The posting agent uses one of these on at least every other post and logs its name in the "trend" field.
Rules: the joke or the useful bit comes first and CertTrack comes second (or not at all). Never invent stats,
fines or customer stories. Most recipes are carousels (slideshow) because carousels are what's working for us.

## Hot right now (seen in Nov 1-8 2026 trend roundups)

### exactly-what-to-say (NEW)
Trend: "When <hard moment> happens, here's exactly what to say: '<phrase>'": a verbatim script instead of advice; top business/marketing format the week of Nov 8 (median ~19x usual reach).
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "When a client asks for your COI and you're not sure it's current, here's exactly what to say:" -> "'Let me pull the current one and send it over today.'" / "Then actually check the policy dates before you hit send." / "If it lapsed: 'Our renewal is in process, I'll send the updated certificate as soon as it's issued.'" Last slide: "Save this for the next 4:55 PM Friday request."
Slide kinds: hook, statement x3 (title = the line to say, body = the note), optional cta. Other scripts: front desk when an inspector walks in early, office manager when a vendor's insurance expired mid-job. Keep it practical; no legal advice.

### money-cant-buy-happiness (NEW)
Trend: "Money can't buy happiness, but..." finished with a small, specific luxury, one per beat (Nov 2 roundup; strong pre-Black Friday).
CertTrack spin: carousel, one "but it can buy" per slide. Hook: "Money can't buy happiness, but it can buy:" -> "A reminder 60 days before the license expires, not the day after." / "Every COI in one folder you can actually find." / "A Friday where nobody says 'wait, when does our insurance renew?'" / "Sleeping through renewal season." Last slide: "Small business luxuries are weird."
Slide kinds: hook, statement x4-5, optional cta. Industry swaps: contractor, salon owner, fleet manager.

### tiny-text-reveal (NEW)
Trend: the real message hides in very small text so viewers pause or zoom to read it (Nov 2 roundup; brands use it for a cheeky opinion or offer).
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "Our office's very calm and organized renewal process:" -> point slide with a big title "Everything is under control." and the body as fine print: "the liquor license renews in 9 days and nobody has the login." / "Our insurance is all set." body: "(we think. it was set in 2024)". Last slide: "Zoom in on your own paperwork before the inspector does."
Slide kinds: hook, point x3 (big title = the calm claim, small body = the tiny confession), optional cta. Caption: "zoom in on slide 2".

### kinda-chic (NEW)
Trend: "Kinda chic to..." captions naming proud but unglamorous habits, 4-6 lines plus one self-deprecating one (Sept meme, still on Nov 1 meme lists; text-over-image native).
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "Kinda chic to:" -> "Know when your business license expires without checking." / "Have the COI ready before the GC asks." / "Renew the vehicle registrations in October, not the week they lapse." / "Have one spreadsheet. Not 'FINAL (2)'." Last slide: "Also kinda chic to find out about it from a reminder, not an inspector."
Slide kinds: hook, statement x4-5, optional cta. Industry swaps: clinic office manager (CPR cards), restaurant GM, landscaper.

Grr Mondays." / "The bond renewal notice was in Friday's mail. Nobody opened Friday's mail. Grr Mondays." / "'Quick question, when does our liquor license expire?' Grr Mondays." Last slide: "Make Monday the day the reminder shows up, not the deadline."
Slide kinds: hook, statement x3-4, optional cta. Post on Mondays only. Industry swaps: restaurant GM, property manager, dispatcher.

## Evergreen formats that keep performing

### pov-panic
"POV: <a specific, relatable moment of panic>". The first slide is the POV line and the rest is the story. Our POV carousel was a top post.

### tell-me-without-telling-me
"Tell me you run a <business> without telling me you run a <business>" -> 3-4 inside-joke slides (e.g. contractors: "4 different COIs for 4 different GCs, all expiring in different months").

### red-flags-green-flags
"Red flags when a business says its paperwork is 'handled'" then flip to green flags. The myth slide kind works well for this (red flag = myth, green flag = fact).

### things-that-just-make-sense
"Things that just make sense as a small business owner": 4-6 short satisfying habits ("renewal dates in ONE place", "a reminder 30 days out, not 3").

### rate-it
"Rating ways businesses track license renewals, 1-10": sticky note on the monitor 2/10, memory 0/10, spreadsheet 5/10 ("if someone opens it"), calendar reminders 6/10, a system that alerts the whole team 10/10. Ends with a light CertTrack mention.

### unpopular-opinion
"Unpopular opinion: <a debatable but defensible take>" plus 2-3 supporting slides, and the caption asks "agree or disagree?". Must be genuinely arguable, not rage bait.

### self-aware-brand (Gen Z marketing parody)
The brand roasts its own marketing: "Our marketing team (it's one guy) said to make a TikTok about license renewals. So here's 3 renewals you're definitely forgetting." Honest about being tiny; that's the charm. At most once a week.

### this-or-that
2-4 slides of "Would you rather: renew 30 days early, or explain to the client why the COI expired?" Caption: "comment your picks".
