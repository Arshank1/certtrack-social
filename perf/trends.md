# TikTok trend recipes for CertTrack

Refreshed weekly by the "CertTrack trend refresh" scheduled task (last refresh: 2026-10-05).
Each recipe takes a format that is getting big views on TikTok right now and gives it a CertTrack twist.
The posting agent uses one of these on at least every other post and logs its name in the "trend" field.
Rules: the joke or the useful bit comes first and CertTrack comes second (or not at all). Never invent stats,
fines or customer stories. Most recipes are carousels (slideshow) because carousels are what's working for us.

## Hot right now (seen in late Sept / early Oct 2026 trend roundups)

### stop-the-wedding
Trend: photo carousel where each slide reads "Stop the wedding, but..." and finishes with a petty, oddly specific objection.
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "Stop the wedding, but make it small business." Slides: "Stop the wedding, but the venue's liquor license expires the day before." / "Stop the wedding, but the caterer's COI on file is from 2024." / "Stop the wedding, but nobody knows who renews the permit." Last slide: "Okay, resume the wedding. Someone set a reminder."
Slide kinds: hook, statement x3-4, optional cta. Industry swaps: restaurant, cleaning, landscaping.

### gold-star-behaviour
Trend: "Gold star behaviour:" followed by an absurdly small win; built to make people add their own in the comments.
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook "Gold star behaviour for business owners:" then one tiny win per slide: "Knowing your license number without digging for it." / "Renewing the COI before the client asks." / "Every renewal date in ONE place." / "Getting the reminder 30 days out, not 3." Caption: "add yours".
Slide kinds: hook, point x4-5. Mostly genuine habits; CertTrack at most as one item.

### real-horror-story (relatable Halloween)
Trend: horror-movie language for everyday frustrations instead of actual spooky content (October).
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "Scariest stories for small business owners (October edition)". Slides: "The certificate of insurance that expired... last month." / "The renewal notice went to an employee who left in March." / "'Who renewed this last year?' (no one answers)." Last slide: "Sleep well. Your dates aren't in a drawer." Use through Oct 31, then retire.
Slide kinds: hook, statement x3-4, optional cta.

### propaganda-im-not-falling-for
Trend: on-screen list of "propaganda I'm not falling for" (and sometimes "propaganda I AM falling for"), deadpan.
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "Propaganda I'm not falling for as an office manager:" -> "'The insurance agent will remind us.'" / "'It's in the spreadsheet somewhere.'" / "'That license is good for a few more years, probably.'" Flip slide: "Propaganda I AM falling for: renewing early and never thinking about it again."
Slide kinds: hook, myth/statement x3, statement, optional cta. Myth slide kind fits the "not falling for" items.

### same-answer-every-time (the "Reasons to get a bob" deadpan list)
Trend: a numbered list where every item is the same two words, delivered deadpan.
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook "Reasons to track your expiration dates". Points 1-4 all say "Surprise inspections." (or "The client asked."), last point breaks the pattern: "5. Also, surprise inspections."
Slide kinds: hook, point x4-5, optional cta. Keep it dry; let the repetition be the joke.

### prized-possession-then-vs-now (American Girl Doll trend)
Trend: childhood "my most prized possession" vs. the grown-up, boringly practical one.
CertTrack spin: carousel. Slide 1 "My most prized possession at 8:" -> "A glitter gel pen." Slide 2 "My most prized possession now:" -> "A certificate of insurance that's actually current." Industry versions: contractor (COI), restaurant (food handler cards), trucker (medical card), salon (state license).
Slide kinds: hook, statement x2, optional cta.

### how-different-our-lives-are
Trend: two contrasting routines side by side, one calm and one chaotic.
CertTrack spin: carousel with a compare slide. "Office manager with a spreadsheet" vs. "office manager with reminders". Left: digging through email, calling the agent, "who renewed this last year?". Right: coffee, a reminder 30 days out, done.
Slide kinds: hook, compare, statement, cta (product-forward: counts toward the product limit).

### worth-the-money
Trend: "things that are actually worth the money", mixing surprising picks with obvious ones.
CertTrack spin: carousel for a niche. "Things worth the money as a contractor: a good ladder, a good accountant, renewing your license BEFORE it lapses, a second set of COIs on file." Mostly genuine tips; CertTrack at most as one item, never the whole list.

## Evergreen formats that keep performing

### pov-panic
"POV: <a specific, relatable moment of panic>". The first slide is the POV line and the rest is the story. Our POV carousel was a top post.

### tell-me-without-telling-me
"Tell me you run a <business> without telling me you run a <business>" -> 3-4 inside-joke slides (e.g. contractors: "4 different COIs for 4 different GCs, all expiring in different months").

### red-flags-green-flags
"Red flags when a business says its paperwork is 'handled'" then flip to green flags. The myth slide kind works well for this (red flag = myth, green flag = fact).

### things-that-just-make-sense
"Things that just make sense as a small business owner": 4-6 short satisfying habits ("renewal dates in ONE place", "a reminder 30 days out, not 3").

### rate-it
"Rating ways businesses track license renewals, 1-10": sticky note on the monitor 2/10, memory 0/10, spreadsheet 5/10 ("if someone opens it"), calendar reminders 6/10, a system that alerts the whole team 10/10. Ends with a light CertTrack mention.

### unpopular-opinion
"Unpopular opinion: <a debatable but defensible take>" plus 2-3 supporting slides, and the caption asks "agree or disagree?". Must be genuinely arguable, not rage bait.

### self-aware-brand (Gen Z marketing parody)
The brand roasts its own marketing: "Our marketing team (it's one guy) said to make a TikTok about license renewals. So here's 3 renewals you're definitely forgetting." Honest about being tiny; that's the charm. At most once a week.

### this-or-that
2-4 slides of "Would you rather: renew 30 days early, or explain to the client why the COI expired?" Caption: "comment your picks".

## Retired / avoid
- how-could-this-day-get-better and saxophones-getting-louder (Sept trends; gone from Oct roundups, no trend-tagged stats yet). Retired 2026-10-05.
- Generated motion videos with a chosen library track as the default format (avg ~15 views, Oct 1-2). They're now only a 1-in-3 experiment.
- The same CertTrack sales line on every caption.
- #fyp / #foryou / #foryoupage: they add nothing and look spammy.
