# TikTok trend recipes for CertTrack

Refreshed weekly by the "CertTrack trend refresh" scheduled task (last refresh: 2026-11-23).
Each recipe takes a format that is getting big views on TikTok right now and gives it a CertTrack twist.
The posting agent uses one of these on at least every other post and logs its name in the "trend" field.
Rules: the joke or the useful bit comes first and CertTrack comes second (or not at all). Never invent stats,
fines or customer stories. Most recipes are carousels (slideshow) because carousels are what's working for us.

## Hot right now (seen in Nov 17-22 2026 trend roundups)

### this-is-your-sign (NEW)
Trend: "this is your sign to [small step]": a hesitant beat, then doing the small thing (Rising, Nov 16-22 roundup; median ~3.5M views). Usually filmed; works as text: the hook is the sign, later slides are the tiny steps.
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "this is your sign to check when your business license expires." -> "not this week. right now." / "find the certificate. look at the date." / "if it's before March, put a reminder 60 days ahead." / "then go back to your coffee." Last slide: "(send this to whoever has the login)."
Slide kinds: hook, statement x3-4 (lowercase, short), optional cta. Swaps: COI, liability policy, food handler cards, DOT/vehicle registrations, contractor license.

### the-urge-to (NEW)
Trend: lowercase caption "the urge to [specific impulse]", a pause, then giving in, no words (Peaking, Nov 16-22 roundup; median ~364K views). Works as a text carousel of relatable impulses.
CertTrack spin: carousel, one urge per slide. Hook: "office manager urges:" -> "the urge to rename 'COI_final_FINAL(2).pdf' to what it actually is." / "the urge to reply-all 'who has the insurance login'." / "the urge to tape the permit expiration date to the front door." / "the urge to check the license date one more time. (it's fine.) (check again.)" Last slide: "giving in to the last one is allowed."
Slide kinds: hook, statement x4-5, no cta needed. Swaps: contractor, salon owner, fleet dispatcher, restaurant GM.

### we-listen-we-dont-judge (NEW)
Trend: "We listen and we don't judge" opener, then one harmless confession per beat (Peaking, Nov 16-22 roundup; median ~161K views). Usually to camera; text-only version is one confession per slide.
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "We listen and we don't judge. Small business owners, go:" -> "I've never read our insurance policy. I just know it 'exists.'" / "Our renewal system is a sticky note that fell behind the printer." / "I found out our permit expired because the inspector told me." / "I renewed the same license twice because I didn't trust I did it." Last slide: "No judgment. Just maybe a reminder this time." Caption: "drop yours below, we don't judge."
Slide kinds: hook, statement x4, optional cta. Confessions must be generic and harmless; no invented customer stories.

### expectation-vs-reality (NEW)
Trend: confident "expectation" then the reality (Peaking, Nov 16-22 roundup; median ~262K views). Text version: two slides per beat or "Expectation:/Reality:" on one slide.
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "Running a small business: expectation vs reality" -> "Expectation: making the product. Reality: finding last year's COI." / "Expectation: 'renewals are automatic.' Reality: the card on file expired in July." / "Expectation: the inspector calls ahead. Reality: Tuesday, 11:40 AM, no warning." Last slide: "Fix the one you can: know your dates."
Slide kinds: hook, myth x3 (myth = expectation, fact = reality) or statement x3, optional cta.

### exactly-what-to-say
Trend: "When <hard moment> happens, here's exactly what to say: '<phrase>'": a verbatim script instead of advice. Still the top business/marketing format on Nov 22 (median ~20x usual reach) and rising on Nov 19 meme lists (often with the wrong version beside it).
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "When a client asks for your COI and you're not sure it's current, here's exactly what to say:" -> "'Let me pull the current one and send it over today.'" / "Then actually check the policy dates before you hit send." / "If it lapsed: 'Our renewal is in process, I'll send the updated certificate as soon as it's issued.'" Last slide: "Save this for the next 4:55 PM Friday request."
Slide kinds: hook, statement x3 (title = the line to say, body = the note), optional cta. New twist: "Don't say: '...' / Say: '...'" pairs (myth kind). Other scripts: front desk when an inspector walks in early, office manager when a vendor's insurance expired mid-job. Keep it practical; no legal advice.

### its-november
Trend: "It's November... you know what that means" then the holiday-season reveal (Nov 13 roundup; holiday music is peaking in Nov 17-22 sources, plus Thanksgiving prep Nov 21-25). Text only: don't name or quote any singer/song or use that audio; let TikTok auto music pick. Retire after Nov 30.
CertTrack spin: carousel. Hook: "It's November... you know what that means." -> "Pie orders." / "Holiday hires who need their food handler cards." / "Finding out which permits expire Dec 31." / "Asking who has the login to renew the business license." Last slide: "It's time. (to check the renewal dates)." Thanksgiving week swap: "Things I'm thankful for as an office manager: ..." list.
Slide kinds: hook, statement x3-4, optional cta. Industry swaps: bakery, retailer, event venue, snow-plow landscaper.

### day-1-survival-story
Trend: "Day 1. You're stuck in..." second-person survival story told day by day (Nov 13 roundup; not in Nov 17-22 sources: last call, retire next refresh unless it resurfaces).
CertTrack spin: carousel, one day per slide, "you" voice. Hook: "Day 1. You're the only one in the office who knows when the insurance renews." -> "Day 3. Someone asks for the COI. You find three. None of them say 2026." / "Day 7. The renewal email went to someone who left in March." / "Day 12. You find the login on a sticky note under the keyboard." / "Day 14. The inspector's car pulls into the lot." Last slide: "Day 15: ..." (cliffhanger; caption asks "what happens next?").
Slide kinds: hook, statement x4-5 (title = "Day N.", body = the beat), no product line on the slides.

## Evergreen formats that keep performing

### pov-panic
"POV: <a specific, relatable moment of panic>". The first slide is the POV line and the rest is the story. Our POV carousel was a top post.

### tell-me-without-telling-me
"Tell me you run a <business> without telling me you run a <business>" -> 3-4 inside-joke slides (e.g. contractors: "4 different COIs for 4 different GCs, all expiring in different months").

### red-flags-green-flags
"Red flags when a business says its paperwork is 'handled'" then flip to green flags. The myth slide kind works well for this (red flag = myth, green flag = fact).

### things-that-just-make-sense
"Things that just make sense as a small business owner": 4-6 short satisfying habits ("renewal dates in ONE place", "a reminder 30 days out, not 3").

### rate-it
"Rating ways businesses track license renewals, 1-10": sticky note on the monitor 2/10, memory 0/10, spreadsheet 5/10 ("if someone opens it"), calendar reminders 6/10, a system that alerts the whole team 10/10. Ends with a light CertTrack mention.

### unpopular-opinion
(Peaking again in Nov 16-22 roundups, median ~1.4M views: good week to use it.)
"Unpopular opinion: <a debatable but defensible take>" plus 2-3 supporting slides, and the caption asks "agree or disagree?". Must be genuinely arguable, not rage bait.

### self-aware-brand (Gen Z marketing parody)
The brand roasts its own marketing: "Our marketing team (it's one guy) said to make a TikTok about license renewals. So here's 3 renewals you're definitely forgetting." Honest about being tiny; that's the charm. At most once a week.

### this-or-that
2-4 slides of "Would you rather: renew 30 days early, or explain to the client why the COI expired?" Caption: "comment your picks".
